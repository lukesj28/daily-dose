Daily Dose Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

1. Who we are

Daily Dose is developed by Lucas San Juan (Canada), who is the data controller for the app. Contact: help@sanjuan.dev.

2. Summary

The developer collects no personal data. Daily Dose has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash reporting. No data is sold or shared.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device: saved articles, your notes and highlights, your highlight colour, your acknowledgement of the disclaimer, and the position of the navigator button. This data is kept locally using SwiftData and UserDefaults and is never sent to the developer. Deleting the app deletes this data. You can delete individual articles in the Library.

4. Network requests

The app makes two kinds of network request:

  • It downloads the daily article (today.json) from GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, Inc. and served through the Fastly content delivery network.
  • It loads figure images from the servers of the U.S. National Library of Medicine (ncbi.nlm.nih.gov).

These providers receive your IP address and standard request data (such as device and user agent information, and the time of the request) as part of normal web traffic, and process it under their own privacy policies: GitHub Privacy Statement and NLM Web Policies. The app adds no identifiers, cookies, or personal data to these requests.

Tapping an article's source link opens the NCBI website in your browser.

5. Legal basis

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR, the developer processes no personal data. Any processing by the hosting providers is based on their legitimate interest in delivering and securing their service.

6. Retention

The developer retains no data. Data stored on your device stays there until you delete it or delete the app.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA (Canada), and CCPA/CPRA (California), you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability, and the right to withdraw consent. The developer holds no data about you, but you can contact help@sanjuan.dev with any request and we will respond within 30 days.

You may also complain to your supervisory authority, for example the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, your EU or EEA data protection authority, or the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

CCPA/CPRA: we do not sell or share personal information, and we collect no sensitive personal information.

8. Children

Daily Dose is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA) and knowingly collects no data from them.

9. International transfers

Hosting providers may process request data in the United States and other countries.

10. Apple

Downloads and App Store data are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

11. Security

All network requests made by the app use HTTPS.

12. Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with a new effective date.

13. Effective date

November 18, 2026.
